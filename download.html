Reassemble the ISO

cat sigmaos-desktop-2026.11.23-x86_64.iso.part* > sigmaos-desktop-2026.11.23-x86_64.iso
sha256sum -c sigmaos-desktop-2026.11.23-x86_64.iso.sha256

Write to USB

sudo dd if=sigmaos-desktop-2026.11.23-x86_64.iso of=/dev/sdX bs=4M status=progress oflag=sync

Warning: replace /dev/sdX with your USB device — this erases it.

Try in QEMU

qemu-system-x86_64 -m 4G -enable-kvm -cdrom sigmaos-desktop-2026.11.23-x86_64.iso

Or with VNC for headless testing:

qemu-system-x86_64 -m 4G -enable-kvm -cdrom sigmaos-desktop-2026.11.23-x86_64.iso -vnc :1
